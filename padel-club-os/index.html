Questions

What owners
ask first.

Do we have to switch our booking system?+

No. Padel Club OS reads your schedule and sends players to your usual booking flow. Today it runs on top of Lunda. Other booking systems are next.

Do players need a new app?+

No. Today players use Telegram, with a club bot, a Mini App and the club chat, plus your website. WhatsApp is next.

How much staff time does it take?+

Staff mostly tap: approve a post, take a request, add a guest to a group. The system writes the drafts, sends the reminders and keeps the log.

Who owns the player data?+

Your club does. Each club gets a separate database for its players and their history.

Which languages?+

The Padel Battle website runs in English, Russian and Armenian. We set up the languages your players use.

What does it cost?+

It depends on the number of courts and the modules you switch on. We share the price on the demo call, after we see your empty hours.
