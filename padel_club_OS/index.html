12
Questions

What owners ask first.

Do we have to switch our booking system?+

No. We integrate your current booking system into Padel Club OS and keep it as one of the sources of clients.

Do players need to download an app?+

No. They use a mobile-friendly web app, or the messengers and bots they already have.

How do you know where a booking came from?+

Every link the club sends out has its own code. When a booking appears in your schedule, the system matches it to the player's clicks and marks it proven, likely or organic.

How much staff time does it take?+

Staff mostly tap: approve a post, promote an empty court, approve a message to a customer. The system pushes admins to do their job.

Do you run tournaments too?+

Yes. Our own tournament system records every match, standings and results. With the honest rating it will track each player's progress, best partners and toughest rivals.

What does it cost?+

It depends on how data-driven your club is, how complex the integration is and how much revenue growth we see. We share the price on the demo call.
